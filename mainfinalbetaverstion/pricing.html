Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI Threat Correlation work?
Our AI engine analyzes patterns across multiple vectors (network, endpoints, user behavior) simultaneously. Instead of surfacing 1,000 isolated low-level alerts, it correlates them into a single high-confidence incident narrative, drastically reducing alert fatigue for Pro and Enterprise users.
Can I upgrade or downgrade at any time?
Yes. You can manage your subscription from the Settings panel in the dashboard. Prorated charges or credits will be automatically applied to your account balance based on your billing cycle.
Does CybeWatch support on-premise deployments?
On-premise deployments and isolated air-gapped networks are supported exclusively on our Enterprise tier. Please contact our sales team to discuss architectural requirements.
